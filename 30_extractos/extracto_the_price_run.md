## 1. Mecanismo institucional del run [PDF: The Price Run, p.1 y p.8]

- "One institutional liquidity provider can start to shift their prices & quotes relative to flows coming in which triggers a market wide response... This response triggering mechanism causes prices to become 'inefficient' and compounds price runs, makes them overshoot, **leaving a vacuum of liquidity behind it**." [p.1]
- "A big (relative) order can cause prices to start moving fast and if other quote providers are offering sell liquidity below the actual bid price in broader market, they will be exploited & arbitraged against — to save themselves from getting clipped they employ infrastructure to keep prices the same." [p.1]
- Detalle del mecanismo [p.8]: "When price is low liq state & a relative surge comes through... independent banks widen spreads slightly & their pricing engines start going... their prices move higher to keep prices the same which subsequently starts to 'suck up' buy flows, the flows start to 'chase' the quotes & are forced to take higher prices. **This results in a thin, inefficient move higher which ultimately overruns as a function of the price chasing mechanism each individual liquidity providing bank employs, leaving a void of liquidity behind.** Price starts to get jumpy towards the top where the flows start to dry off."
- "The liquidity providers' job is to execute orders & fill them as quickly as possible whilst protecting their bottom line. Providers split big blocks into smaller pieces to match them with counterparties as efficiently as possible without materially affecting the market." [p.8]

## 2. Volatilidad y volumen [PDF: p.2 y p.4]

- "Liquidity (passive & active) is not a constant, volume is variable ie doesn't come in consistently in the same size all of the time. **Price goes to the weaker, less liquid side.** Due to this price can move against the broader sentiment/trend even if it isn't a move with conviction." [p.2]
- Caso GBPUSD julio 2019 (9 días de chop, sentimiento bajista constante) [p.4]: "Did market sentiment shift & chop around that many times? No... Sell volume wasn't 'constantly' pushing lower, rather it continued to nudge it down 'consistently'. **Each run higher on cable was a function of weak sell liquidity, liquidity seeking on buy flows.**"
- "**Volatility. Is. A. Function. Of. Available. Liquidity.** If there's a void of offers above price and bids are coming in thicker (even slightly thicker), price will move through that weak area. Vice versa." [p.4]

## 3. Low Liquid State y disparo del run [PDF: p.5 y p.7]

- "Almost endlessly price goes into a low, stable volume area of trading — referred to as **low liquid state (on all timeframes)**, which is subject to a shot of liquidity/volume on one side of the market. You may have heard **contraction leads to expansion** — we're looking at why & how price expands through the prism of liquidity & liquidity filtering. (More on Low Liq State in Price Delivery folder)." [p.5] → LLS se desarrolla en el módulo Price Delivery.
- "Volume isn't constant, the state of volume/liquidity shifts throughout the day, week, month, year, economic cycle & often dries off. For example: market open, close, through Asia, futures open, before & after data, central bank shots." [p.5]
- Esquema "Relative Price Stability Shift — triggers of price runs" [IMG: p.7]: "Low volume/stable volume range; liquidity drawing away from around the stable range; **increased volume/surge on one side — the 'increase' is relative, doesn't have to be massive, just more than the volume keeping price stable.** Often the increase in volume is NOT massive hence why bids dry off quickly."

## 4. El run desde el punto de vista del volumen [IMG: p.6, esquema hipotético 5min]

- Rango estable con volumen 5–10 → "Order, Buy, 100 (only 5-10 available offers inside stable volume range, low liquid state)" → el precio corre buscando los 100; en el camino solo encuentra offers de 10 y 1–6.
- "Buy inventory dries off (100 = filled), liquidity providers job done. **The way price came higher left liquidity vacuum, bids dry off & price starts to rebalance lower looking for liquidity. The leg up has thin levels of liquidity.**"

## 5. Price spike y agotamiento [IMG: p.9, p.10]

- "This spike doesn't necessarily need a big shot of volume. Just a **relative spike on one side vs the preceding conditions/available liquidity**. Hence why spikes often dry out quickly & don't follow through — it can be a small shot which dries out quickly **returning back to base**." [IMG p.9, GBPUSD t5]
- Secuencia anotada completa [IMG p.10, GBPUSD 5min]: "Relative volume stability → 'relative' volume surge → **price run** → drying off, bids exhausted, liquidity shift & rebalance lower → **price rebalanced lower through liquidity vacuum back down to core base & responds**."
- "**How do we know where price will dry off?** We take a deeper look later but price responds at liquidity bases on opposite side of market, has relative behaviours & price features we look for to determine drying off/exhaustion & liquidity shifts. **Its around these areas we can look to take trades & exercise & model setups around.**" [IMG p.10]

## Aporta al destilado

- Base causal del método: el overshoot ineficiente no es anomalía sino consecuencia mecánica del price-chasing de los LPs → todo run fino deja vacío detrás y tiende a rebalancear a la base core.
- "Relative volume surge": el disparador de un run es RELATIVO al estado previo, no absoluto — clave para no esperar volumen masivo.
- Ciclo canónico del run: estabilidad → surge relativo → run fino → agotamiento (jumpy top, bids exhausted) → rebalance por el vacío → respuesta en core base.
- LLS (Low Liquidity State) queda apuntado aquí y desarrollado en Price Delivery.
