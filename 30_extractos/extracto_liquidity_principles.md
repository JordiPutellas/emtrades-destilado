## Los 9 principios (transcripción fiel) [PDF: p.1–2]

1. "Volatility is a function of available liquidity"
2. "Price goes to the weaker/less liquid side"
3. "Price leaves residual liquidity, liquidity is transferred as price moves along" *(→ C-001 en 91_contradicciones.md)*
4. "3 sides to a liquidity base — The top, middle & bottom"
5. "HTF bases have more liquidity pooled inside them vs LTF (Liquidity = friction)"
6. "Price simply seeks, finds & deals off liquidity"
7. "Extensions get filled — The further price travels inefficiently the weaker it gets"
8. "Price runs & swings are fractal, occur all day every day, some price runs are smaller ranging from a few pips all the way to HTF 1000+ pip price swings"
9. "Efficient price delivery is very strong until it isn't"
