## El continuum [PDF: Liquidity Imbalance continuum]

- "A downward (or upward) move can be generated by **2 core conditions on either side of the spectrum**:
  1. **Exhaustion on the bids** (for downward moves) after leaving a liquidity void/inefficiency
  2. **Selling pressure**
  Regardless of what price is doing it is moving from one end of the continuum to the other, virtually all of the time." [p.1]
- Lado exhaustion: "Price extended inefficiently leaving liquidity vacuum in its wake & ultimately moved down after bids dried up... The move down is a function of weaker & weaker bids and **the natural state of selling in the market is enough to rebalance price back down**." [p.1]
- Lado pressure: "A down trending move with consistent selling, a move down with conviction — which we would expect after price puts in high, deals off buy inventory & **moves below apex base causing a shift**." [p.1]
- En tendencia bajista: "Through the duration of selling there will be pockets of buyside liquidity which cause price to move higher rebalancing weak extension legs (offers drying out). But because this is a selling move, **each run higher finds sellside liquidity and pushes price down to new lows repeating the extension, fill, extension, fill pattern**." [p.1]
- Uso con CPS: "If you take one of the UP moves rebalancing the extension leg (fractal price run), one can zoom in and **use CPS features to determine selling continuation points** — this way you tilt probabilities in your favour, avoid selling breakouts & exploit fractal price swings to trade the broader context. You HAVE to (be) sure that price is moving down through weak levels of bids (mid price run) & the characteristics of the down move are that of a downtrending/midflow market." [p.1]
- Tendencia alcista (espejo): "Every time price extends, bids dry out & price draws down rebalancing through the weak leg & finds more liquidity close to price. Why is buy liquidity close to price? **Price put in low, picked up buyside liquidity & caused a shift when it moved through sell inventory.**" [p.2]

## Aplicación a gestión [PDF: p.2]

- "**When you take a trade you want to know which end of the continuum you're taking a trade on for trade management purposes** — basically, so you're not left holding your bollocks shorting an extension and holding till kumbaya **when the move down was just filling a weak leg**."

## Aporta al destilado

- Marco de diagnóstico binario (con espectro entre medias) para TODO movimiento: ¿rebalance por agotamiento o presión con convicción?
- Consecuencia directa de gestión: un short sobre extensión agotada es un trade de FILL (target = la pierna débil), no de tendencia — no mantenerlo como si fuera presión.
- Conecta CPS/midflow con el continuum: los puntos de continuación se buscan cuando el precio rebalancea una extension leg dentro de un mercado midflow.
