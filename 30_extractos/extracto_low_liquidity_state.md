# Extracto — Low Liquidity State.pdf

> **Fuente:** `00_fuentes/pdfs/Price Delivery/Low Liquidity State.pdf` (9 diapositivas). Define formalmente el LLS anunciado en The Price Run.

## 1. Definición [PDF: Low Liquidity State p.1, p.2]

- "During various situations liquidity can (draw) out of the market where less volume is being transacted, this leaves price sensitive to a price move. Times like **end of day, Asian session, ahead of data, market uncertainty**. During these times less volume is transacted & less liquidity is filtered into the market → **lower tick frequency** & price movement **contracts into a low liquid state**." [p.1]
- Mecanismo del pop [p.2]: "All it takes is a little bit of weight on one side, as NEW incoming volume that's heavier than available liquidity AT THAT PERIOD OF TIME, which 'triggers' price & liquidity seeking algorithms to fill the new order... **The liquidity imbalance doesn't have to be huge or long lasting, just bigger than the volume being transacted in the LLS**, which leaves the NEW run out of the range prone to drying off & rebalancing back to base = price run. When price is in a LLS naturally we can expect a pop out of it (**contraction leads to expansion explained**)."

## 2. Ciclo perpetuo [PDF: p.3]

- "The move out of the LLS was inefficient as price is seeking liquidity, leaving liquidity void behind. Bids dry off, there's no more buyers to continue pushing, price bleeds lower looking for liquidity again. **A rinse and repeat process, a cycle, in perpetual motion on all timeframes because of low liquidity state.**"

## 3. Fractalidad del LLS [PDF: p.5; IMG: p.4, p.7]

- "LLS can have broader fractals with smaller pieces inside them that look similar to the overall LLS area = fractal, patterns of self similarity on different scales." [p.5]
- "Remember price is most subject to a move, **even against the trend**, when in LLS — either side of the market." [p.5]
- Daily GBPUSD [IMG: p.4]: "Square = low liquid state fractal. This is HIGHER TIMEFRAME LLS, generally when liquidity drops off it is because of **macro environment and sentiment**, maybe ahead of data & central bank speaks. Notice when price is trending: price goes into a LLS, pops out and forms a new LLS, pops out again. **Price move, sideways, price move, sideways is trending structure, on ALL timeframes.** The longer price is in a LLS the less volume is being transacted which generally means price will have a **BIGGER move** out of the LLS. Notice when price runs out of the LLS price becomes inefficient, leaving that inefficient leg exposed to rebalancing. Anytime price is moving inefficiently it is looking for liquidity & once that run dries off it leaves price vulnerable to a rebalancing."
- GBPJPY H4 [IMG: p.7]: "Low liquidity state at previous low liquidity state = fractal" (varios casos marcados; los LLS nuevos se forman sobre LLS previos).
- "The same price move seen on different timeframes: see how price is largely in a LLS on some scale most of the time and every time it runs out the price run creates opportunities." [p.8]

## 4. Things to remember [PDF: p.9]

- "Price goes into a LLS around certain times (ahead of data, market close, Asian session); liquidity also draws away from outside the LLS range."
- "HTF LLS is macro-environment related, generally when the market is waiting for direction or a policy shift."
- "In LLS price is most subject to a price run on EITHER side, even against sentiment = volatility is a function of available liquidity, price goes to the weaker side."
- "**We want to do our business whilst price is in the low liquidity state, anticipating which side is weaker & thus where price is likely to break** (more on this during trade examples & trade management)."
- "**Price tends to range 80% of the time and move 20% of the time** — due to this prices generally draw back into ranges."
- "**Low liquid state = opportunity not only before the break but during and after = price run & rebalances.**"

## Aporta al destilado

- LLS pasa de mención a definición completa: contracción por retirada de volumen/liquidez, con horario (EOD, Asia, pre-data) y versión HTF (macro).
- El trigger del pop es el mismo "relative surge" de The Price Run, formalizado.
- Estructura de tendencia = LLS → run → LLS → run (move, sideways, move) en todos los TF.
- Regla de localización operativa: hacer el negocio DENTRO del LLS anticipando el lado débil (desarrollo prometido en trade examples/management).
- Estadística citada: 80% rango / 20% movimiento → el precio tiende a volver a los rangos.
