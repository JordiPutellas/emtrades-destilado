## Contenido propio

- "Variable volume flow = **volume is not a constant**." [IMG: Variable Volume Flow p.3]
- Esquema + Cable 1min [IMG: p.3]: rango con "void of offers (above), bids continue to come in → **price pops out to find the next pocket of orders & ultimately draws back in the range**." (Coincide con el principio de que el precio tiende a volver a los rangos.)
- Cuándo es el precio más susceptible de moverse [PDF: p.4]:
  - "Price can enter low liq states anywhere & everywhere, **generally after pickups of liquidity, apexes**. Also Asian session, market close, during price pauses of a broader price run."
  - "Important to understand conceptually when price is most subject to a move & why. This will help you **refine your areas for trade entry & help avoid chasing price**."
- Dos usos operativos del LLS [PDF: p.4]:
  1. "When price is in a low liq state, it may be forming in a desirable place ie **after picking up a nice buy base with weakness on sell side — you can look to take trades there in anticipation of fill/run higher**."
  2. "OR one can look to **fade the actual extension out of the liquidity base, as the price run dries off** — for example after price has ran a fair bit, **extended past a high into a feeder = potential short**."
- Disclaimer del propio material [PDF: p.4]: "*note* this isn't explaining HOW we will trade this, rather purpose is to explain how, why & when price is most subject to a price run on either side of the market from a volume standpoint."

## Término nuevo

- **Feeder**: usado sin definir ("extended past a high into a feeder = potential short"). [PDF: p.4] **Actualización M2020 S14:** resuelto en lo esencial como el nivel original al que el precio alimenta, prioritario sobre su pickup. [VID-M2020: Session 14 @ 00:00:41–00:01:02]

## Aporta al destilado

- El LLS aparece también tras pickups de liquidez y apexes, y en pausas de un run mayor — no solo por horario.
- Dos plantillas de trade alrededor del LLS: anticipación del break desde base deseable, o fade de la extensión al secarse el run.
- Vocabulario: feeder (nuevo, sin definición).
