## Secuencia intradía numerada (6 pasos) [IMG-MIET: Vol is a F of Av Liq 1] (GBPUSD 5min, 1-2 abr 2020)

1. "Price cleared out liquidity from level on way out — **washed**." 2. "Residual pocket — visible on LTFs. Book cleared of offers in pocket from pickup." 3. "**Asia range — thin volume, light liquidity, low tick frequency.**" 4. "**Surge of volume as LPs open their books ahead of London open.** Liquidity already light and LPs forced pull flow higher seeking counterparties → thin illiquid move. Price jumps through previously cleared pockets to area of liquidity." 5. "Next pocket of liquidity cleared, **opening the book up**." 6. "**Reload on bids — book is clear and open**, market reprices higher aggressively in search of offers at next area of core liquidity."
- Novedad operativa: el ciclo horario Asia (LLS) → **apertura de libros de los LPs en London open** como disparador estructural del surge.

## Dislocation/Rebalance en pares [IMG-MIET: Vol is a F of Av Liq 2] (5min)

- Cajas verdes etiquetadas "**Dislocation**" seguidas de cajas rojas "**Rebalance**" — cada dislocación lleva emparejado su rebalance. Vocabulario de etiquetado sistemático para tracking.

## Mecánica completa de LPs en 7 pasos [IMG-MIET: Vol is a F of Av Liq 3; versión tweet: 4 twit] (GBPUSD H4, 9-abr-2020, post-low 1.14095)

1. "Buy limit inventory." 2. "Aggressive market buying pushes price out... **LPs (can) compound move higher by (1) warehousing — potentially stabilising offers coming through whilst bids pushing — and/or (2) widen spreads competitively in response to other LPs, sucking up the market buy flow.**" 3. "Thin move higher meets residual sell liquidity, aggressive bids tapering off & **LPs unloading any inventory**. Illiquid move leaves **bid vacuum** behind, exposing price to move lower even on light selling." 4. "**Reload on bids off top of inventory** — market reprices higher and grabs sell liquidity, opening up the book." 5. "Surge of sell volume, bids disappear. Reprices aggressively lower back to bid inventory, leaving behind **offer vacuum**." 6. "Price reloading on bid inventory, aggressive selling drying off and limit bids absorbing. Consequently light market buying coupled with offer vacuum = **seamless move higher**." 7. "**Ranges above ranges** as result of limit buys accepting higher prices alongside market buyers pushing on price, in a broadly weak area of offers."
- Tweet adjunto (9-abr-2020): "whilst on aggregate, for cable move higher since low, **liquidity has come in one sided (buy limits X market buying vs relatively thin sell liquidity)** — that doesn't mean its constantly coming in the same direction and same intensity, **it often tapers off**."
- Términos nuevos: **warehousing** (LP almacena inventario para estabilizar), **bid/offer vacuum**, "ranges above ranges".

## Tweets doctrinales [IMG-MIET: Vol is a F of Av Liq 7] (2020)

- "**Liquidity = limits; market orders take liquidity; volatility, then, is a function of available liquidity. Stable liquidity is result of ratio of market to limit orders being (broadly) balanced, often as result of volume drying.**"
- "**State of market has significant bearing on which style of trading is effective or not.**" (= la tesis de entornos de Session 13, en versión tweet 2020.)

## Corrección doctrinal: qué es una ineficiencia [IMG-MIET: InNeFFiciencY] (tweet 4-may-2020)

- "Been seeing newer guys sharing charts (incorrectly) identifying small skinny legs inside broader moves. Its clear there's big gap in conceptual understanding of what a inefficiency represents. **The whole move is inefficient, not just some 3 pip skinny leg from 143 hours ago.**" Dibujo: flechas burlonas "iNeFfIcIeNcY" sobre skinny legs sueltas vs llave verde "**Whole fucking move is inefficient**".
- Réplica de EM: "Rushing to use new buzzwords without understanding what they reflect is NUMBER ONE & 265th reason traders are shit."
- → Refina la entrada PI del glosario: la ineficiencia es una propiedad del ESTADO/movimiento completo, no de micro-gaps aislados. Coherente con "escala relativa" y con S13 (environments).

## Mapeo de régimen [IMG-MIET: One sided vs stable] (GBPUSD H4, tweet 22-abr-2020)

- "**Black = one sided liquidity w/ different levels of intensity. Orange = stable liquidity.**" Cajas alternadas sobre el V COVID. Tweet: "clearly, liquidity has stabilised over last month give or take. **Ahead of next move**."
- → El mapa one-sided/stable es la práctica del "assess the state of liquidity" como capa previa al trade.
