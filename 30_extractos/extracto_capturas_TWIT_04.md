## EMTRADES 1-2 (9-abr-2020, GBPUSD H4) — el mecanismo LP en 7 pasos anotados

- [IMG-TWIT: EMTRADES] y [IMG-TWIT: EMTRADES 2] son **idénticas**. Chart H4 de la recuperación post-COVID (1.14095 → 1.24) con secuencia numerada sobre el gráfico:
  1. "**Buy limit inventory**" (caja verde — la base).
  2. "Aggressive market buying pushes price out, seeking next area of offers, **inside of an existing weak area of sell liquidity** (red box). LPs (can) compound move higher by **1 warehousing** — potentially stabilising offers coming through whilst bids pushing — **and/or 2 widen spreads competitively** in response to other LPs, sucking up the market buy flow."
  3. "**Thin move higher seeking offers meets area of residual sell liquidity** (red lines), aggressive bids tapering off & LPs unloading any inventory. Illiquid move higher **leaves bid vacuum behind**, exposing price to move lower even on light selling (orange box)."
  4. "**Reload on bids off top of inventory** — market reprices higher and grabs sell liquidity (yellow ellipse), opening up the book."
  5. "**Surge of sell volume, bids disappear.** Reprices aggressively lower back to bid inventory, **leaving behind offer vacuum** (blue box)."
  6. "**Price reloading on bid inventory**, aggressive selling drying off and limit bids absorbing. Consequently light market buying coupled with offer vacuum = **seamless move higher**."
  7. "**Ranges above ranges** as result of limit buys accepting higher prices alongside market buyers pushing on price, in a broadly weak area of offers."
- Tweet: "whilst on aggregate, for cable move higher since low, liquidity has come in one sided (buy limits X market buying vs relatively thin sell liquidity) **that doesn't mean it's constantly coming in the same direction and same intensity, it often tapers off**."
- → La ilustración más completa del ciclo warehousing→vacuum→reload→dealing range en un chart real. Clasificación (b, mayor).

## Emergency Cut (3-mar-2020, recorte de emergencia de la Fed) — LA EXCEPCIÓN AL FADE

- [IMG-TWIT: Emergency Cut]: "Something as profound as emergency cut = **liquidity disappearing almost straight away and it doesn't have to reappear for a while**. Effect is sharp moves, loads of ticking, easy to push market around... price ticks more as market orders push price further (vs normal conditions) to find liquidity. This environment is unique in FX: **you can chase price, long as you get the significance of catalyst (emergency cut) on available liquidity.** GBPUSD sell liquidity is extremely thin as result of **LPs become averse to holding USD inventory** and not having enough GBP inventory to match bids with. It's perfect environment for LPs to even **lift the offer, take opposite side of non speculative GBP sellers and hold the risk**. Result is **open book to the upside**, light market buying vs lack of sell liquidity = **seamless drift higher with ranges above ranges even on light volume. Amazing price behaviour and market state to chase price, trail stops, buy the retest of highs.**"
- → Regla de excepción explícita: con catalizador de calibre suficiente sobre la liquidez disponible, el playbook se invierte (chase, no fade). Complementa "sorpresa grande = repricing sostenido" [PDF: Liquidity Void p.11] con las tácticas concretas.
