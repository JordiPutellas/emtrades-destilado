## Mecanismo void→rebalance numerado sobre oro [IMG-MIET: Gold Ranges 1.0.jfif, 1.1.jfif] (H1, 31-mar/1-abr-2020)

1. "Pickups of liquidity and subsequent responses '**clear the book**', creating area of weakness/thin liquidity."
2. "Shot of volume against existing area of weakness initiates liquidity seeking move to fulfill active flow. '**Fast' liquidity (HFTs, PTFs, LPs) becomes averse**, liquidity further disperses well past the existing weak area."
3. "Series of price levels where liquidity draws away from active sell flows resulting in relatively thinly traded (per price level), **illiquid move through the gap. If move is event driven, effect is more pronounced & has longer duration.**"
4. "**Market rebalancing liquidity — takes much less weight to push price back up as result of thin move lower.**"
- Es la chain reaction de Liquidity Void.pdf con actores nombrados (HFTs, PTFs, LPs) y el matiz event-driven cuantificado en duración.

## Dealing ranges y metaorders [IMG-MIET: Gold Ranges 5.1.jpeg] (H1, 2-abr-2020)

- "**New dealing range where liquidity has stabilised again, below previous one.** Price holding below often result of '**slow' but larger liquidity (institutional), building out positions over a period of time (metaorders, usually automated)**, having to accept less favourable prices (as more aggressive traders push price) to transact/find counterparties. In this case, **move sell limits lower to find bids stepping in beneath previous range**."
- Términos nuevos: **dealing range** (rango donde la liquidez se estabiliza; hay captura homónima en EM Twits) y **metaorder** (orden institucional grande troceada en el tiempo). Dicotomía fast liquidity (HFT/PTF/LP) vs slow liquidity (institucional/metaorders).
