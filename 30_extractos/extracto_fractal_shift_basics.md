## Enfoque anticipatorio [PDF: p.2, sobre GBPUSD Daily/H4/H1]

- "When liq drops off & price goes into low liq state it is most vulnerable to a price run. **We want to find the preceding patterns & behaviours which tell us a run is likely to form, which side the run is likely to move in, so we can position ahead of the move — taking an anticipatory approach. That means you won't get confirmation**; rather we look at the anatomy in price, how it's moving, the stage price is in & the relative parameters for those stages to gauge probabilities."
- "Each one of these runs will leave **micro pgaps & inefficiency which will impact later price movements — tempo**."

## Aporta al destilado

- Declaración metodológica central: el método es **anticipatorio, sin confirmación** — se posiciona antes del run leyendo anatomía/fase/parámetros relativos.
- Las micro-ineficiencias que deja cada run condicionan el "tempo" de los movimientos posteriores.
