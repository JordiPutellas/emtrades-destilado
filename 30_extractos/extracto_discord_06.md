## [DISCORD: Disc 64, 65, 66] — ⚠️ CONVERSION RATE: definición formal

- "Latent liquidity lol. **Hidden liquidity. Which is much much more than visible. Look at transacted volume per day vs all the posted visible liquidity — there's ALWAYS more volume transacted than the total visible liquidity net. Because most of the liquidity is hidden. To avoid information leakage.**" [Disc 64; comparando DOMs de futuros 6B/6E]
- Definición (harry: "define conversion rate, what to what?"): "**The rate the latent liquidity converts into orders. If it doesn't, there's only top-of-book liquidity plugged in by market makers — hence you have an open book.**" No es agresividad: "no. It's the rate of conversion of hidden liquidity into orders. **Very fast rate as price approaches level = they'll absorb the move. Little to no conversion = open book.**" Drivers: "further you go out, more liquidity turns up — **unless that conversion rate slows down for whatever reason. Uncertainty usually**... conversion rate is where you get **skewed latent liquidity, or a skewed book**"; "**directly linked to conviction/certainty/willingness to transact. FX has high levels of natural demand → a pretty evenly distributed latent book over time — hence the large large ranges.**" [Disc 65]
- Aggressive ≠ esperar tu precio: "if they were aggressive they wouldn't wait for their price — they'd populate the top of book spread"; passive willing → "they can become aggressive **and populate the book in one direction whilst the opposite remains open — cable March this year. That's one-directional liquidity in a high urgent environment. EURUSD 2014 is similar — almost unbelievable.**" Y el matiz histórico: may-2018→ene-2020 fue "**consistent selling rather than constant**". [Disc 66]
