## Guerrilla process [PDF: Process Over Profits p.2]

- Analogía: fuerza convencional (big players, reglas dictadas por su tamaño) vs insurgencia. "The smaller unconventional force often has the advantage because it can establish a broader definition of what winning is... **An unconventional force can win by just not losing.**"
- "We are all small fish. Accept it, deal with the cards you're dealt and **develop a process to survive**."

## Propósito del proceso [PDF: p.3]

- "Identify a way to consistently execute, identify when to execute and when not to, how to pick favourable odds."
- "Your results are a function of how well you've prepared, your level of understanding. **It's your job — nobody else's.**"

## Process building model (5 etapas) [PDF: p.4]

1. "Your current level of understanding/reality."
2. "Exploring the market for what it is — the framework for how price develops, characteristics & opportunities & why they're present."
3. "**Engage these price behaviours relative to your understanding, deliberately.** Force yourself to experience, analyse & observe price for what it does. Engage a specific component of price."
4. "Do it enough your brain starts to internalise those experiences relative to that specific component. Internalising makes it second nature — skill & confidence building."
5. "Continue to refine & reach expert level of proficiency in that particular aspect. **Reach expert level in one aspect = move to the next whilst executing the first.**"

## Refine your engagement process [PDF: p.5]

- Elegir UN componente de precio (ej: "clean extensions, inefficient price delivery into a key unfilled liquidity base"). Cada vez que aparece: (1) "gauge the context in price (where price is on price swing) first of all"; (2) "consciously explain how this price behaviour relates to the liquidity principles, what you expect to happen and why"; (3) "observe how price developed, the characteristics & the sequence of events".
- "Do it enough and you will refine a process on **what needs to happen when price forms this exact behaviour, what it shouldn't do, time of day, small nuances, the specific parameters for that specific model**."
- "**Build a sample set of 10 a day — after a month (22 trading days) that's 220 structure experiences.**"
- "Not only do I know the parameters, I understand the variations... I have a feel, an intuition for how price puts in highs/lows = **second level information that can't be taught or passed along**."

## Aporta al destilado

- El "trade model" de EM se construye por componente aislado, con muestras deliberadas (10/día), hasta internalizar; luego se apila el siguiente componente.
- Encaja con Fractals _ Scaling p.8: primero exercising amplio, luego especificar el comportamiento a operar y formar parámetros (entrada, ejecución, desarrollo, gestión, hora del día, timeframe).
