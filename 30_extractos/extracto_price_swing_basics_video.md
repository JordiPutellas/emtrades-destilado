  - **Price Swings Inside Price Swings**: "We want to know where these price swings are forming, off what levels, if they're midflow swings or a price swing at a high or low when price is turning around. Being able to distinguish where the fractal swings are forming, you're better able to connect with the tempo of price movement & you're then able to use the fractal swings inside the broader one to NAVIGATE through price, increase probabilities for trade entry whether with the flows or against, with varying duration for trades in each respective scenario."
  - Ejemplo midflow alcista: "Price is midflow higher, price runs out of local liquidity base, leg dries off & price rebalances down to nearest inventory on the buyside. In this instance, if I was shorting, it'd be shorting the extension past previous high & to fill it back down to base — **counter flow trading, short holding times on trades**. We could also use these fractal price swings to get onboard the broader swing higher... wait for price to rebalance down to local liquidity base & look to trade the context, in this case long — **midflow trading**."
  - Ejemplo midflow bajista: "Price is midflow to the downside, we would expect each price swing higher to pickup sell liquidity & continue pressuring lower, extending past the base until sellers dry out/price hits thick enough level to cause price swing higher. Note, we want to consider 'HOW' price moved down in context of efficiency, to determine if the leg is vulnerable or subject to a rebalance."

## Aporta al destilado

- Fractalidad operativa: los swings LTF dentro del swing mayor se usan para NAVEGAR y elegir entrada, a favor o contra flujo, con duraciones distintas.
- Dos modos con nombre: **midflow trading** (a favor del flujo tras rebalance a base local) y **counter flow trading** (fade de la extensión de vuelta a base, holding corto).
- El "CÓMO" se movió el precio (eficiencia del tramo) decide si una pierna es vulnerable a rebalance — enlaza con el módulo Price Inefficiencies.
