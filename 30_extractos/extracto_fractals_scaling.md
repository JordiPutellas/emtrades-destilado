## 1. Fractals [PDF: Fractals _ Scaling p.1]

- "Liquidity movements & price surges occur in similar patterns on all timescales... multiple smaller price surges on a declining scale (ie move less) yet have similar features and characteristics to the whole."
- "We can use fractal swings for **timing and feel of tempo** of any single move with difference between scales being **distance & time** related — **anatomy is the same**."
- "Study of patterns of smaller scales must consider what part they play in the larger timeframe — this helps you trade broader context / get bigger moves / avoid bad trade management."

## 2. Scaling [PDF: p.7]

- "As timeframe is reduced the chart looks the same fractal wise (runs), we see the price behaviours repeated at every scale but **the volatility also reduces relatively — somewhat proportionately**."
- "Important to study price behaviour **in context of volatility to its timescale**. HTF pockets have more liquidity thus will generate greater volatility in responses vs the fractal patterns inside the broader legs which give us KEY information for trade decision making & management (**we're looking for weakness to trade into or strength to trade with**)."
- "Intent is to familiarise yourself with CPS; through that you can establish areas of high probability for trading, swing stage characteristics for management & **gauge dynamic probabilities as a move develops**."
- "Will form **template like approach** for what price should do in certain areas if it's likely to play out in certain way **vs what it shouldn't do**."

## 3. Resumen [PDF: p.13]

- "**Price reflects traders behaviour — traders repeatedly do the same thing thus price reflects this on all scales & is reason for fractal nature of price.**"
- "All price behaviour is fractal (PIs, price swings & features, ranges, responsive areas) regardless of sentiment or direction."
