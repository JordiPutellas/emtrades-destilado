## [DISCORD: Disc 80] — el edge se acorta: anticipación > confirmación

- EssFX: "**in electronic markets today the edge is much much tighter and presents itself for shorter periods. Those who wait for confirmation are trading where the markets too efficient. Over time their returns erode away. So anticipatory approach > responsive.**" Especialmente en FX, "a broadly balanced market in last 5 years".
- Define "eficiente" aquí como ausencia de edge: "**the move where probabilities are asymmetric (and there's a distortion present in mkt) has been and gone**"; cuando el trader entra tras confirmación, la información ya está embebida y "**the dislocation has been repaired**".
- Breakouts: "**MOST breakouts aren't breakouts**". Se pueden identificar variaciones de condiciones donde el breakout sea probable, "**but doesn't happen often**". No contradice el enfoque anticipatorio: la excepción se identifica antes; esperar la ruptura como confirmación llega tarde.

## [DISCORD: Disc 81–89] — eficiencia, balance y poder predictivo inmediato

- Mecánica de la distorsión: "**You have entities top of book responding to supply and demand devoid of underlying asset. The behaviour of traders causing distortions**"; los traders responden al propio precio y amplifican esa distorsión. En mercados menos líquidos puede durar (ejemplo diamantes); en mercados reales más activos/voluminosos "**these inefficiencies are balanced quicker**". [Disc 81–83]
- Proxy discute el vocabulario de supply/demand y fair value; son objeciones del alumno, no doctrina. EssFX mantiene que supply/demand es medible y que supply restringida + upside abierto tiene capacidad predictiva; valida la cadena buying/selling→distorsión, no la eliminación conceptual de supply/demand. [Disc 83–84]
- Formulación de edge micro: "**In the absolute immediate term it is the ONLY predictive power, especially considering the transactional methods in markets. Limit order protocol vs market order protocol. This top of book dynamic of liquidity responding to orderflow and other liquidity creates distortions for very short periods of time, hence why price goes against the mass sentiment on light volume.**" [Disc 84]
- Randomness: "**Distribution of outcome isn't equal chance of outcome, in a skewed environment**" y "**randomness doesn't have to be equal chance either**". Las asimetrías observables pueden coexistir con un mercado aleatorio. [Disc 85]
- Eficiencia en mercados líquidos: suelen reflejar valor hasta que aparece una distorsión reflexiva o, en corto plazo, el landscape de liquidez se sesga por order flow; esta segunda distorsión "**repeating and repairing quickly**". Desde el prisma de liquidez, eficiencia/balance = "**short term buy side liquidity and sell side liquidity having similar distribution**". Intentar comprar o hacer trend trading en balance → "**you'll get chopped up**" / "**you'll do your arse**". [Disc 85–86]
- Objetivo declarado: "**We're not fair value traders. I'm saying distortions in liquid markets are repaired quickly creating opportunity. And I'm trading market back to a balanced state.**" [Disc 87]
- Definición compacta de EssFX: "**Inefficient = divergence from value. Value in markets = balanced liquidity. We look at mechanics creating these inefficiencies short term. And long term distortions also. Without inefficient markets = you can't trade.**" [Disc 88]
- Por qué unfilled/core liquidity tiene poder predictivo: "**Core liquidity remaining down at price 1. Price diverges from 1 to 10 because of adverse selection risk, liquidity continues to pool at price 1. That's predictive power... Find the liquidity, find the value.**" [Disc 88]
- Cierre epistemológico: la discusión se atasca por definiciones distintas; EssFX propone "**From now on we have to define words**". [Disc 89]
