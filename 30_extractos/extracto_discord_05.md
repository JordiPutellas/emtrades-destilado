## [DISCORD: stop cascades.jpg + Disc Misc.jpg] — mecánica MM y el driver del rebalance (6-may-2020)

- EssFX: "A conventional MM on a centralized exchange earns the spread. But it has to account for two things: **adverse selection** — getting picked off and filling informed traders (which leads to **toxic inventory**); and **inventory risk** — MMs try to be risk neutral and minimize excess inventory. This affects price: a MM is likely to increase spread when it can determine there is orderflow against it — might withdraw or quote wider during a price run. **After a price run it might have excess inventory, being on the wrong side. So it might have to unload by aggressively skewing bid/ask or quoting one-directionally.**" → mecanismo microestructural del rebalance post-run.
- A Proxy: "**id argue LPs have capacity, in fx, to offset toxic flow as price runs in a direction and even accumulate inventory in direction of move cus of information advantages OTC FX offers dealers. Less so stop cascades.**" (Otra vez: stops relegados.)

## [DISCORD: Mirosctructure.jpg + Inefficiency conversation.jpg] — qué es microestructura + procedencia Lori

- EM: "**Microstructure isn't a study of how effective technical analysis is — it's regarding the efficiency of a market**... How the market **embeds information into price** by looking at variables affecting it — **from market organisation to transactional systems**... market is imperfectly efficient — **it's what creates opportunity**." Ejemplos: "dealers **hot potato trading** (aggressively offloading a customer's flow between them) creating bigger market impact than would normally"; ventajas informacionales del fast money → "huge effect on price efficiency, **where and when you see price 'break', volatility pickup**."
