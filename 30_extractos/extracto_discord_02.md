## [DISCORD: Disc 13] — "liquidity doesn't move price" + tipos de trader grande

- **Corrección a Manel** (doctrina de primera): "btw **liquidity doesn't move price, it's the market orders consuming it. So when you're seeing price hold inside a base it's because there's liquidity there — it's the only thing holding price.** Duration of hold, broader context and flow are all things to consider. But price holding in a base and saying there's no bids there is incorrect."
- GrifoneTrader pregunta si una entidad con bids empujaría con market orders para agotar la liquidez contraria más rápido. EssFX: "**You probably want to avoid finding causal and specific reasons for why [the market] does what it does — markets too random and complex. Hence why observing it against a prism of governing principles.**" Y luego: "Depends on type of trader and execution protocol. **Most big traders are passive, ie dealing with limit orders — they'll chunk out a big order and it'll get filled over course of several hours if we're talking in the billions. THAT is more significant than pretty much all other traders**... Other participants have informational edge: if book is heavy on bid side cus of big iceberg order, it's not impossible any offers are lifted or internalised by LPs — **that opens the book up. Then any market orders coming through = price easily runs to less liquid side.** But again, it's overly specific — loads of things can happen."
- GrifoneTrader: "focus, then, has to be on differentiating between market states" → EssFX: "yeah — in a single 50-pip move theres so many different changes in orderflow, **but on aggregate price is in a specific state. and then those states vary far and wide.**"

## [DISCORD: Disc 18, 19, 20] — ⚠️ MECANISMO POR TIMEFRAME (aclaración mayor)

- EssFX: "**Let's clarify: the LP-responding-to-flow dynamic really applies to intraday/immediate timeframes.** [Gold weekly/daily] is **autocorrelated flow continuing to trade in the same direction — the fast money effect or micro volatility isn't observed on these timeframes.** I'd be surprised if LPs avoiding adverse selection and moving quotes aggressively lasted more than 60 seconds — although it can happen sequentially over a 60-minute period."
- Qué observa un LP: "**LPs only care about the next few minutes**... it's the **depth of the book + volatility + spread — that's what they're observing.**" Qué mueve el largo plazo: "**sentiment, or whatever macro models they're applying to determine value**... Long term moves are functions of slower liquidity aka **latent distribution** — the normal digesting forces that don't turn up."
- "It's wrong to assume the gold rally has had a constant depth reflecting bid strength for 100 weeks — **collective opinion of informed traders + urgency**... depth of book isn't constantly reflecting fundamental value just like sentiment isn't a constant pressure — **thus LP behaviour isn't relevant on HTFs; it's more to do with latent liquidity distribution.**"
- "**forecast =/= LP behaviour**... **i have a 1.50 forecast but the next 5 mins matter to me, because current state doesn't reflect my forecast**... LP is managing risk more than worrying about forecast... **Plainly: fast money isn't relevant on higher timeframes because they don't operate there. What IS true though: price is fractal and similar behaviours are observed.**" ¿Por qué no cotizan según forecast?: "**they can't just provide liquidity in line with their longer term forecasts because they'll be exposed to huge drawdowns — price doesn't go up in a straight line.**"
- Prácticas turbias: LPs filtrando info a otras divisiones, "FX cartels negotiating client flow for front running", consorcio de pricing en Singapur ("might eventually turn into a global central exchange for fx"). Freno: "**regulation + client relationships — OTC fx is predicated on bank-client relationships.**"

## [DISCORD: Disc 21, 21.1] — visible vs real orderbook; latent liquidity y conversion rate

- Esquema a mano de EssFX: "**Visible orderbook vs Real orderbook**" (el real con órdenes mucho mayores escondidas más lejos) — "effect more pronounced in centrally traded products but **information game is played in all markets**".
- "In long term moves that hidden order book balance is skewed. **Traders hide orders to limit information leakage.** If you looked at visible top-of-book liquidity in sp500 vs executed volume per day, it wouldn't match up — much more volume trades vs available liquidity → **participants hide orders and only reveal after they've been clipped or as price approaches levels — conversion rate. Conversion of latent liquidity is a huge influence on LP behaviour or subsequent price behaviour.**"

- trader_umr: los niveles de futuros funcionan "to pip perfection" en futuros y difieren 10-15 ticks del spot. EssFX: "volume data... **fx prices aren't formed on futures exchanges**.
