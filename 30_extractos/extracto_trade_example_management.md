# Extracto — Trade Example _ Management.pdf

> **Fuente:** `00_fuentes/pdfs/Process/Trade Example _ Management.pdf` (8 diapositivas). El documento MÁS operativo de todos los PDFs: un short real de GBPUSD (31/07/2019, pre-FOMC) con entrada, stop, parciales, invalidación y compounding. **Aparece SWE en fuente** y la lista de response areas de alta probabilidad.

## 1. La tesis del trade [PDF: Trade Example p.1]

- "Short idea was primarily based on **how price ran up**: price ran through weak areas of sell liquidity in an exposed way, seeking fills on buy flows (right into a H1 pocket of sell liquidity). We know inefficient price runs are subject to drying out and rebalancing."
- "Principle: the further price runs the weaker it gets. Price ranges around 80% of the time, so whilst people are expecting material follow through we're looking at probabilities for buy flows to fade."
- "**Short executed at 1.22466 with 10pip stop.**"

## 2. Contexto y tracking [IMG: p.2]

- "A core part of the process is **tracking price as it develops, tracking liquidity & categorising 'how' price is moving**, seeing how it fits inside the framework (price swing)."
- Anotaciones de la subida previa: "Entire H1 fractal liquidity base / only unfilled part of the fractal, topside / price picked up daily buyside liquidity / **sell inventory holding price down aka shelf — we saw price invalidate it** (red circle) / bids pushing past sell inventory increases probability for a run higher / whatever sell liquidity pushed lower (or buy flows drying out, **LIC**) was absorbed by newest pickup of buyside establishing higher low, further increasing probabilities for run higher / **low liquid state at weak levels of sell liquidity (we know this because sellers were absorbed at the new pickup of buy liquidity & higher low established)** / red arrows illustrate **transfer of buyside** higher & higher."
  - → Muestra CÓMO se anticipa el lado débil de un LLS: evidencia de absorción (vendedores absorbidos + higher low) ⇒ lado vendedor débil. *(Responde parcialmente pregunta 14.)*
  - → Usa lenguaje "transfer of buyside" (2019) — relevante para C-001.
- "As a function of internalisation... I start anticipating a fill of this skinny, inefficient extension leg."

## 3. First touch counter flow short [PDF/IMG: p.3]

- "An initial short was taken on the premise that price will rebalance an exposed leg down to shelf & ultimately continue higher. This is because of 'WHAT' price had recently done — price put in low, dealt off shelf, higher low, shift etc."
- "**Any trade against the recent shift is often just to rebalance a weak leg into local buy inventory — I wouldn't be expecting a full blown reversal & new lows. The only instance I would expect new low is if the bounce preceding the shift picked up residual liquidity**, but in this instance we grabbed daily buyside." → regla de expectativa/target del counter flow.
- Anotaciones: "First touch short taken here. As it stood, the run didn't look complete rather this leg looked exposed, therefore short was taken on the **left end of the Liquidity Imbalance Continuum — Bid Exhaustion**. / Closest buy inventory, short collapsed inside here. / **If price started to show signs of breaking down further I can always take shelf flip trade.** / As it stood, the price swing was complete so any short entry was categorised as a counter flow."

## 4. Short principal: constituent features del run [PDF/IMG: p.4]

- Secuencia numerada sobre el gráfico (las "constituent features of a price run"): "**1/ Fractal base → 2/ Apex Retest → 3/ Shifting point, price released through weak but unfilled sell liquidity → 4/ Price pause → 5/ Continuation leg/parabolic phase — weakest part of the run**."
- "Short executed as price ran quite a fair bit and the constituent features of a price run were all present. At this point I had more context for the price run potentially topping out."
- "I don't make these kind of calls rather I just trade liquidity relationships — price goes to the weaker side. This short was again executed on the **left end of the LIC spectrum**: bids exhausted, price remained inefficient & also extended. A confluencing factor: the constituent features of a price run (price pause etc) showed up."
- "Point of release of H1 fractal with parabolic phase (point 5) also extending (**extensions get filled**)."

## 5. Gestión: "Paying for the trade" [PDF/IMG: p.5]

- "Short executed at 2246.6, **stop ~10 pips** at 2256.6. Just like anyone, my balls are in my mouth but I know its just a trade."
- Plan: "First things first, I'm looking for price to breakdown towards shelf. **At the shelf, I never expect price to materially break through it and go into midflow without picking up the liquidity inside there (remember: it's the inventory of buyside) & dealing it off / getting absorbed. Only time I will expect price to move through the shelf straight away is if it is WASHED & even then expect some stalling & absorption.**" → conecta con SWE (Shelf Wash Extension [USUARIO]).
- "Apex & shelf of fractal price swing at high: this flipping & price breaking past **doesn't mean a shift of the broader price swing, rather it opened up a run down to the shelf**." (Fractal vs broader: el flip del fractal solo habilita el tramo hasta el shelf mayor.)
- **Parcial**: "Price ran down, hit the shelf, **I secured equity paying for my trade & the risk assumed on the trade**. It was EXTREMELY important for me to do this to manage my psychological profile & to be able to sustain price coming back on me, which ultimately happened. Another reason: market & price were waiting for FOMC."
- "Sometimes I won't pay for the trade, sometimes I will — **depends on what kind of trade it is, merits of the trade & event risks**." [PDF: p.5]

## 6. Gestión: dejar correr, compound, invalidación [PDF/IMG: p.6]

- "**Stop moved down to invalidation = high had to hold for sell idea to remain valid. Not always will I move my stop down, but again event risk.**"
- Compound: "Whatever remaining liquidity was picked up from the shelf was absorbed inside the high — buyside is weak up here. **Depending on my conviction in the idea, how weak the shelf was (if price invalidated it or not), the nature of the level price is picking up, how far extended it is & surrounding data risk, I would consider taking another short here as compound.** Again, in this case not worth it due to event risk."
- "Continued selling, follow through, shifting point... **Here, I stop managing so tightly & let the trade run, expecting midflow characteristics in price.**"
- "**Through trade management I am constantly shifting between 1-5-15 min timeframes for a broader look.** Take away 'WHAT' I expected to happen as price developed & my invalidation points."
- "**At this stage, my invalidation is price bottoming out at a shit mid price run level. Was unlikely due to time of day, being 1900HRS BST.**"
- Añadir en tendencia: "If one was entirely confident, they could add to shorts where we're seeing continued selling — **I usually would wait for a rally (fractal price run off residual into sell inventory to fade)** but in this instance we had a strong catalyst. **CONTEXT MATTERS, ALWAYS.**"
- "FOMC reaction = catalyst." HTF version: "If this was a daily run higher with the fractal run being H1 — only difference is relative volatility & time. In such a case (HTF), **I would definitely look for compounds & midflow trades beneath the shelf**. We will discuss trade models, parameters including shelf flips & momentum trade later on."

## 7. Final notes [IMG: p.7]

- "Offers drying, price picking up bids but sellers materially pushing on them (**right end of the spectrum, strong pressure**). If one was taking longs down here, we would be looking at 'HOW' price is responding: **failure of bounces, sellers absorbing buyers — NOT characteristics of lows**."
- "Only trades here are counter flow rebalances of weak extension legs, but **nature of timeframe limits how much of a response you're going to get**."
- "One could zoom into these bounces, see price come into sell inventory & get absorbed, CPS features there to exercise continuation... If this was HTF, you bet I'm going long & short."

## 8. Skill development: response areas de alta probabilidad [PDF: p.8]

- "Start out observing & looking for responses everywhere, then look for areas where responses are highly likely with specific parameters, for example:
  - **Liquidity base with an inefficient run into the level**
  - **Apex retest after shelf invalidated**
  - **Mid price run level pickup with an inefficient leg into it**
  - **Extension past previous highs into feeding liquidity**
  - **Swing retest of apex base**
  - or any combination of highly probable features"
- Calibración de variantes: "**You have a liquidity base, price runs inefficiently into it extending past highs after a SWE — high probability. Price moves efficiently into a sell liquidity base — low probability.**" ← **SWE citado en fuente** (sin expandir; [USUARIO] = Shelf Wash Extension).
- "As you do this you will refine your parameters — you want to know the highest probable variation for that specific setup and the low probability variations."
- "Build sample sets... the more refined this 'feel' becomes. This process makes you the trader who does not need validation from anybody else."

## Aporta al destilado

- Primera evidencia numérica: stop de 10 pips en un fade 15min/H1.
- Reglas de gestión concretas: parcial en el shelf ("paying for the trade"), stop a invalidación estructural (el high debe aguantar), invalidación = bottoming en nivel mid-run, compound condicionado (convicción, debilidad del shelf, nivel, extensión, event risk), soltar la gestión al confirmarse midflow, multi-TF 1-5-15.
- Regla del shelf: nunca esperar ruptura directa sin dealing/absorción salvo shelf WASHED (→ SWE).
- La lista explícita de response areas de alta probabilidad = semilla de los trade models.
- Diagnóstico LIC aplicado dos veces (ambos shorts en el extremo bid-exhaustion).
