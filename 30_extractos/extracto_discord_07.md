## [DISCORD: Disc 67–71] — cripto: liquidez, costes ocultos y qué operar

- Proxy comparte un artículo de Deribit sobre cripto vs infraestructura FX. EssFX lo rechaza: "**That article is bollocks lol**"; "**A less liquid market does wild shit and will probably take big chunks from people on the wrong side**"; "**Trading costs are high in crypto. Cus of that. Effective (hidden) trading costs**". [Disc 67]
- Ante la supuesta ventaja de una matching engine rápida: "**But the markets illiquid. The speed of matching engine doesn't matter. It won't provision liquidity properly**"; añade que "**wash trading is big on these exchanges**". Proxy aporta que Bitcoin estaba fragmentado entre exchanges y combinaba alta volatilidad, alto apalancamiento, liquidaciones y mucho retail; EssFX reclama un venue principal y supervisión, señalando wash trading/manipulación en exchanges descentralizados. Distinguir: las afirmaciones de Proxy sobre estructura son de alumno; EssFX confirma iliquidez, provisión deficiente, wash trading y necesidad de supervisión. [Disc 67–69]
- Valor/refugio: EssFX observa que BTC "**hasn't behaved like a haven for the last 10 years**" y se niega a construir una tesis de valor: "**I'm not a value investor**", "**Nor can I move the market — too little edge**". Su regla operativa: "**It's ideas and speculation, can take 10 years to be right. Trade the volatility. Plus price will certainly show if a real bidder yields a sentiment shift.**" [Disc 70–71]
- El resto (blockchain, regulación, política monetaria y welfare) es opinión macro/general, no doctrina del método y no se integra en los maestros salvo la evidencia específica para BTC.

## [DISCORD: Disc 73–78] — poor high/low: definición mecánica y corrección a market profile

- Sobre un low de EURAUD: "**Not a well traded low down there, will have unfinished business. Even if EA runs to 1.68 it's coming back down. Probabilistically anyway.**" Preguntado qué significa: "**Not a lot of trade was facilitated down there basically. Offers dried up. Thing with fx it can leave weak lows in for a while.**" Fresh liquidity más allá del low: "**not necessarily, may or may not be there**". [Disc 73; charts D1 y M5 fechados 3-jun-2020 en Disc 73.1/73.2]
- Mecánica mínima: "**A buyer needs a seller. No seller = no trade. Market order causes price reach for seller leaving thinly traded area behind.**" [Disc 73]
- Harry propone erróneamente que poor high/low implica mucho trade al nivel. EssFX corrige varias veces: "**it's when momentum dries off at peaks**"; "**Little trade facilitated is poor high low. Or exhaustion basically**"; "**Price tends to trade through poor high low not at it**". El recorte de texto y el market-profile chart son material externo/tercero, usados en la discusión, no doctrina independiente de EM. [Disc 74.1, 75, 75.1]
- Aclaración frente a single prints/TPO: "**you can have a poor high single print without excess. Without trade being facilitated there. It's a high that forms due to volume drying off**"; "**high volume doesn't equate lots of facilitated trade**"; "**volume needs liquidity**". [Disc 76]
- Comportamiento posterior: "**It's always a weak low cus no real matching happens there. Even if bids stepping in**"; "**a repair of poor low can be a simple retest. More times than not it'll trade through it**". No asumir que la liquidez queda esperando indefinidamente: "**Chris H thinks liquidity lies in the book for 20 years. It doesn't.**" [Disc 77]
- Traducción del vocabulario de auction/market profile al prisma de EM: "**VP is just complicated version to describe simple trade matching**"; preguntado si hay algo especial en matching/auction/poor lows: "**not really, it's just exhaustion**". La causa de la unfinished auction es "**there wasn't enough liquidity to transact there. Hence price moves away. Thus it's low volume/exhaustion**"; el retorno también puede atravesarla si los bids no son fuertes. [Disc 77–78]
