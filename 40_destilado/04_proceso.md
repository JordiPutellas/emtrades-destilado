# Proceso (EMTrades — destilado)

> Proceso de aprendizaje y flujo de trabajo del trader según EM. Pendiente de completar con el módulo Process y los vídeos.

## Aprendizaje: el "3 step exercising objective"

- "Go through the 3 step exercising objective to internalise price, form a deep connection with price swing stages, how price moves, how it behaves in various environments including data events & time of day, week, year etc. Once confidence is there can start to isolate aspects of price to build trade models (later)." [PDF: Price Swings Continued p.16]
- Los 3 pasos no se enumeran en los PDFs procesados; pistas: steps 1 & 2 = ver/marcar price swings "in definition"; step 3 = "broad non-contextual exercising". Tras el paso 3 se distinguen features LTF vs HTF y se incorporan los liquidity principles a los parámetros de selección de trades. [PDF: Price Swings Continued p.11]
- Método de práctica sugerido: "keep doing marking price swings and you'll start to see these features in more detail, more definition on price". [IMG: Price Swings Continued p.9]
- EM pide validar afirmaciones en vez de aceptarlas: "Needs your own validation, go out and test this rather than rely on face value" (sobre el apex que aguanta dentro del shelf). [PDF: Price Swings Continued p.8]

## Lectura en tiempo real (mencionado, por desarrollar)

- Saber DÓNDE y CUÁNDO un run HTF probablemente se seca: HTF pocket of liquidity + anatomía del top/bottom (parabolic phase, etc.). [IMG: Price Swings Continued p.14]
- "Drawing & refining levels of unfilled liquidity will be covered later" → módulo Process (Tracking Unfilled Liquidity Presentation.pdf). [PDF: Price Swings Continued p.12]
- El "CÓMO" se mueve el precio determina la gestión del trade y las expectativas. [IMG: GBPUSD 5min, módulo Price Inefficiencies]
